# Assembly and physical acceptance

All dimensions mm. This is a design/manufacturing **package**, not a print or
plate order authorization. D-037's Fly connector-model qualification limit
remains accepted as recorded; validate real USB mating at assembly.

1. Choose the matching 16 or 17 pitch parts. Print trays floor-down; splice
   plates with their flat underside on the bed; cable rack inverted on its
   crossbar. The support-post STEP/STL retains assembly Z coordinates: translate
   Z−1.20 to put the peg tips on the bed, print upright with collar support if
   needed. Use PETG or another insulating material. Ream/clean sockets to
   Ø3.10 without deepening them beyond 0.80; leave ≥1.20 of floor.
2. The Ø3.15 free-state peg is split by a 0.60 slot. Its 0.025 radial interference
   is an intentional compliant press fit in Ø3.10 sockets; the installed model
   depicts its compressed shape. Test a fit coupon. Reject cracking or floor
   distortion. Do not force a solid unslotted Ø3.15 peg into the floor.
3. Fit six posts per Wing at the CSV coordinates. Ø3 shaft contacts are at
   Z10.00 and the Ø4.5 collars at Z2.00–2.50. With the PCB resting on the four
   original bosses, gauge **0–0.02 mm** unloaded contact gap at every post;
   trim/shim the removable post tops to the actual boss plane, without preload.
   CAD gaps are 0.00 mm. This is a physical acceptance measurement too.
4. Power off. Check L/R and cavity 1. Pre-mate each underside GHR-14V-S to its
   SM14B-GHS-TB while the unswitched Wing is lifted, slide horizontally north
   through 8 mm free approach and push the housing, never the wires. Dress the
   individual exits south through the fan-in; the 14 wires have OD≤1 mm each.
   Lay the gathered maximum-Ø5 bundle in the R5/Ø6 wall passage. Feed Main's
   end into the central bay as the Wing seats. Keep the floor routing at centre
   Z5 and the elevated Main approach at Z28.5. The rise leans from (±7,−16,5) to
   (±7,−19,14) before crossing the retained rack at Y−19. Keep the14 wires in
   the specified3/4/4/3 flat pack, maximum4 mm inY at the5 mm rack gap, with
   soft lacing below/above the rails. Check0.5 mm nominal clearance each side.
   A round5 mm jacket cannot use this passage. Do not trap wires under posts.
5. Match the exported **160 mm longest-conductor planning route**, including
   the continuous service detour, not an unconnected storage ring. #235's
   reference longest conductor is 123.395 mm; the integrated direct reference and actual dressed service allowance are
   recorded in dimensions.json (the rack detour changes the source reference). 153.4–170 mm is the candidate adjustable range, subject to the
   independent range-endpoint verification and actual wire dressing. Final
   cut/contact datums and length are deferred to fabrication. This package
   makes no 200 mm storage claim.
6. Fit four 3.50 mm POM spacers (Ø3.8 / Ø2.2) per Wing, plate, 0.30 mm nylon
   washers and M2×12 polymer-compatible screws at H1–H4. Tighten evenly just
   to seating. The screw's modelled thread core is Ø1.6; the Ø2 nominal crest
   intentionally forms the Ø1.7 pilot. These eight screws replace the original
   eight M2×6; they are not eight extra PCB fixing sites. Confirm the actual
   selected screw head is within the model envelope Ø3.4 ×1.3.
7. Seat all 21 switches fully with both clips engaged. Check plate thickness,
   aperture fit and switch feet seated on PCB top Z11.20. Gauge the **actual
   fully depressed** keycap skirt at every key: ≥0.30 above plate and ≥0.40
   above fixed case, leaving ≥0.30 after the 0.10 loaded-movement allowance.
   The supplied cap shells are acceptance envelopes, not source cap geometry;
   they cannot establish that a purchased cap profile passes this gauge.
8. Check the complete south band at the rim/plate seam: record maximum vertical
   step and seam gap using a straightedge/feeler gauges. CAD nominal step is
   0.00; nominal gap 0.20. Remove burrs, sharp edges and proud parts. No screw
   lies in the palm shelf. Physical surface coplanarity requires fitting the
   printed ledge to the accepted sheet, and remains an assembly acceptance test.
9. Apply 1 N at every key, measuring PCB and adjacent housing separately with
   dial gauges: differential deflection ≤0.10 mm, key-to-key spread ≤0.07 mm.
   Verify full return, Hall stability, inversion equivalent to 5g, and supported
   keycap removal without plate lift. These are physical tests, not CAD results.

For underside unlatching, power off, remove the plate/switches and lift the
Wing before releasing the latch. For plateless fallback remove plate/spacers,
refit the original M2×6 and 0.5 washers; posts may remain. The raised case rim
requires renewed cap-to-case clearance acceptance in that fallback configuration.
