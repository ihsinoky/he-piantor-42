# JLCPCB Japan support/sales response — 2026-10-05

Provenance: response received by the PO on 2026-10-05, with technical content
supplied in [Issue #51](https://github.com/ihsinoky/he-piantor-42/issues/51)
and the task instructions. This sanitized paraphrase is not a verbatim quote
or a reproduction of the original correspondence. Personal contact information
is omitted.

- The NPTH-to-copper minimum manufacturing capability is **0.20 mm**.
- The supplied nominal values of approximately **0.2001 mm** and **0.2349 mm**
  numerically satisfy that published minimum.
- **0.20 mm is explicitly a minimum capability value**, not comfortable
  production margin or approval of this footprint.
- Final manufacturability depends on the actual Gerber/drill data and the
  manufacturing review associated with an order.
- JLCDFM is not the manufacturing data used by the factory.
- Displaying an NPTH file under **Plated drill layers** in JLCDFM does not by
  itself mean the holes will be manufactured as PTH.
- JLCPCB recommends additional NPTH-to-copper clearance where design permits.
  It did **not** specify 0.25 mm as a recommended target.

The existing [published capability reference](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities)
is retained from C4B3; this increment records the supplied response rather than
claiming a new independent website check. The **0.25 mm preferred target** and
**0.001 mm numerical review guard** are project-owned, not JLCPCB requirements
or recommendations. No production approval or production exception is granted.
