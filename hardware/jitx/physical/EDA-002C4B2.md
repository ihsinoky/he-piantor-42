The reproducer restarts runtime 4.4.2, removes only the ignored generated
qualification design directory and the corresponding `designs/c4b2/run-N`,
then builds, captures and exports sequentially for N=1 and N=2. Historical

for this fixture. Original C4B source/config hashes are verified against its

The underlying commands for each run are:


## DRC interpretation and supported-workflow blockers

Both raw reports record **147 unconnected items, four hole-clearance errors,
and 68 library warnings**. This is not a green manufacturing DRC.

The 147 unconnected items reconcile exactly per net with source physical-pad
islands and historical endpoint accounting. Each Hall net has one missing
connection to its mux endpoint, after subtracting the realized Hall/probe path.
USB_SHIELD has three missing links between its four physical stakes, consistent
with C4B's explicit singleton physical-pad-review limitation. GND has 47,
V3V3 24, HALL_5V 12 and VBUS eight missing links; other per-net counts are
recorded in the reconciliation. Empty CC1/CC2 declarations and accepted NCs
are not converted into false routes. No unexpected net membership was found.

**Blocker 1: source clearance.** J1 contact lands A1_B12/A12_B1 have approximately
0.2001 mm copper-to-locator gaps; A4_B9/A9_B4 approximately 0.2349 mm. These
are below the unchanged fixture's 0.25 mm copper-to-hole qualification rule.
Independent source geometry agrees with KiCad's measurements. They are newly
observed source-rule conflicts, not evidence of copper lost in conversion.
Historical C4B checked pad overlap/containment but did not establish this DRC.
The accepted manufacturer model and historical evidence are preserved.

**Blocker 2: thickness conversion.** Source and exported physical stack total
1.20 mm including masks (1.17 mm excluding them), but generated PCB
`general.thickness` is 1.6 mm. KiCad's Gerber job also declares
`BoardThickness: 1.6`. A fabrication consumer could receive the wrong thickness;
this is a material supported-export discrepancy, not harmless metadata or
partial routing. No supported correction was established in this qualification.
No generated-file patch, design change, upgrade or private workaround was tried.

The 68 `lib_footprint_issues` warnings say the generated library is not included
in the current configuration, despite a generated table and `.pretty` tree
being present. An additional supported invocation with Docker working directory
`/input` returns the same findings. Board-embedded geometry/net mapping checks
pass; library registration/checking remains a limitation. No manual library
repair was performed. There are no additional track, via, board-outline or
copper-clearance findings in these reports. DRC is subject to the generated
project's rules; this does not prove every JITX fabrication field transfers.

## Two-run determinism

[Determinism evidence](c4b2-evidence/determinism.json) accounts for all 86 run
files: 68 byte-identical; 12 differ only in verified timestamp fields; two
copper Gerbers differ in timestamps and ordering of the same two via flashes;
one PCB differs in via record order/IDs; two schematic companion files differ;
one runtime log differs in PID/socket URI. No archive is used in this pipeline.

Every raw difference is retained in
[two-run-differences.json](c4b2-evidence/two-run-differences.json), a read-only diff
that is never applied. No nondeterminism is hidden. PCB comparison preserves
scalar coordinates and polygon/stack/layer sequence, removes only UUID/tstamp
and numeric net aliases, and compares unordered property/object records.
Gerber comparison removes only the two creation-time fields and sorts only
consecutive absolute-coordinate D03 flashes with identical surrounding modal
state. It never sorts paths, apertures, attributes or polarity commands.

**No manufacturing-significant difference was found in this pair:** geometry,
net identity, layer content, drilling and assembly coordinates repeat. The
thickness error repeats too. Position CSVs are byte-identical; drill commands,
tool diameters and coordinates are identical after removing creation times.
DRC differs only in its date. Schematic symbol allocation/presentation and
legacy symbol-library content vary substantially; their semantic equivalence
is unqualified and out of scope for this PCB-fed CAM path. This is a two-run
observation, not a general determinism guarantee.
