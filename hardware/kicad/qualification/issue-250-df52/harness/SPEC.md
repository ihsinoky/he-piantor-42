# DF52 candidate harness and case interface

Study specification, not an order or adoption. Two independent 14-conductor links per keyboard. Each end is DF52-14P-0.8C with fourteen DF52-2832PCF terminals, mating to DF52-14S-0.8H(21). AWG28 tinned copper, UL1571 thin-wall, insulation OD **0.50–0.60 mm**; minimum conductor area for the electrical budget **0.0804 mm²**. Hirose lists 7/0.127 mm construction and 0.9–1.3 mm strip length; use the applicable Hirose crimp tooling and inspect crimp height/pull strength to its tooling instructions. No generic thick-wall AWG28 substitution.

Every electrical conductor is finished **195 ±5 mm, absolute maximum 200 mm**, measured along the conductor between its two mated electrical contact reference points, including terminal/conductor transitions and the **30.2 mm service reserve**. It is not a 200 mm cut-wire length plus terminals or reserve. The case path excluding that reserve must fit 164.8 mm nominal / 169.8 mm absolute maximum. This specifies a length bound; it does not certify the unprovided Phase B route or storage geometry. Inspect every conductor, including the outermost dress path. Case Phase B must verify the actual centreline lengths and reserve storage before an assembly release.

Wire cavity **n to cavity n**; verify with a numbered continuity fixture, not a visual “straight/reverse” vendor label. Cavity 1 HALL_5V; 2/4/8/10/12/14 six independent full-length GND conductors; 3/5/6 MUX_A0/A1/A2; 7 WING_EN; 9/11/13 MUX_OUT_A/B/C. Main J3 retains its existing sheet-local enable name MUX_EN1. No cable position MP: the header's two MP pads are mechanical lands only. No combining the six returns inside the harness.

Seat horizontally and confirm the seating witness. Wires leave parallel to the mounting surface. Keep the first **10 mm from the rear wire exit straight and flat**, at 0.80 mm cavity pitch; do not pull upward or gather against the housing. Place the first independent strain restraint at that 10 mm point, supporting the wires without loading the friction-retained housing. The 10 mm dress is a study constraint, not a manufacturer bend-radius guarantee. Later gathering, a procurement-qualified wire bend radius, ties and the 30.2 mm service loop belong to the case route acceptance. Do not put a 5 mm gathered bundle at the connector root.

`geometry.json` supplies explicit 4×4 local-to-native transforms, exit vectors, bounding boxes and hashes. `mated-and-first-dress-local.step` is a conservative **13.40 ×4.10 ×1.75 mm** mated reservation plus fourteen **0.60 mm OD ×10 mm** first-dress cylinders. The six named endpoint STEPs instantiate it for Main J2/J3 and all four Wing variants. Units are mm. These are reservation solids, not detailed Hirose STEP models.

Local X follows the numbered row (cavity 1 at X=−5.2); +Y is mate/exit, +Z is away from the mounting surface. The local mated bounds are X±6.70, Y−1.26…+2.84, Z0…1.75. Native board top is Z=0, bottom Z=−1.2. Wing models are flipped to B.Cu, with their bodies and wires below Z=−1.2. The case consumer must apply its assembly mounting/Z datum; no unprovided B3 roof solid has been invented. Wing planar transforms are referenced exactly in the JSON.

No connector pose changes were made. At the supplied B3 datum, Main retains +1.90 mm nominal roof clearance, **+1.40 mm after the 0.50 allowance**, exceeding +0.30 mm. Wing retains the **B=6.70 mm** underside reservation and stated plate-top Z12.90 datum. First dress uses no more height than the mated connector. These are inherited-datum envelope checks; enclosure collisions, tie geometry, first-dress interference, lifetime and prototype wire dressing still require Phase B verification.

Electrical budget: 10 mΩ initial DF52 contact per end, plus an explicitly conservative **40 mΩ per crimp termination** pending confirmation of the unavailable B2/B3 numeric allowance. The sensitivity CSV also evaluates 0/10/20 mΩ crimp allowances. Both ends and one forward plus six parallel return paths are counted. The 125°C copper corner is numerical sensitivity only; DF52's catalogue operating range ends at +85°C. Prototype acceptance must cover assembled resistance, crimp quality, rail transient behaviour and analog performance.
